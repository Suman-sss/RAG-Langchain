# Platform Overview

## Vision

Build a staged enterprise AI platform that evolves from grounded document intelligence to multimodal operational support and eventually to agentic orchestration.

## Why This Platform Exists

Enterprise teams deal with fragmented information across documents, screenshots, diagrams, and operational artifacts. This platform demonstrates how to build controlled AI systems that retrieve, reason, and assist with traceable evidence.

## Levels

### Level 1
Enterprise Knowledge Assistant

### Level 2
Multimodal Support Triage Copilot

### Level 3
Agentic Incident Resolution Orchestrator

## Platform Themes

- Retrieval-augmented generation
- Enterprise knowledge access
- Multimodal preprocessing
- Observability and MLOps
- CI/CD and deployment thinking
- Agentic workflow orchestration
