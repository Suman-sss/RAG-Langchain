Employee Handbook

Employees are expected to complete mandatory onboarding within the first 10 business days. The onboarding process includes device setup, HR policy review, manager introduction, and security awareness training.

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local office time unless the employee's contract states otherwise. Teams may follow approved flexible schedules with manager alignment.

All employees must use company-approved communication and file-sharing tools when handling internal information. Sensitive information should not be shared over personal messaging platforms.
