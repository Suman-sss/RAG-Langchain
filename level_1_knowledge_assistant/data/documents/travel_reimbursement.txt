Travel Reimbursement Policy

Business travel must be pre-approved by the reporting manager. Employees should use the approved travel booking workflow when flights or hotels are arranged through the company.

Expense claims must be submitted within 10 business days of travel completion. Receipts are mandatory for hotel, flight, and local transport reimbursement.

Meals are reimbursed according to city-based policy limits. Expenses outside policy limits require written approval.
