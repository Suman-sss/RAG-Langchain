# Interview Questions: Level 1

## RAG Fundamentals
- What is retrieval-augmented generation?
- Why is RAG preferred over sending all documents to the model directly?
- What problem does chunking solve?
- Why do we need embeddings?
- What does a vector store do?
- How do you reduce hallucinations in a RAG system?

## Architecture
- Why separate loaders, retrievers, and prompt builders into different modules?
- Why keep the first version local-first?
- How would you extend this into a production system?

## Multimodal
- How can images be included in a RAG system without direct vision reasoning?
- What are the tradeoffs of using OCR or image descriptions?
